How a price moves one step

The price is simply the last trade. The market maker never trades on its own; it waits around the last price. Each time someone takes one of its quotes, the last price jumps to that quote, and the next re-quote centres on it. Real numbers from the engine, 5 levels shown per side.

Several of the 12 levels round to the same tick, so some prices hold several quotes and the walls get thick. Ticks follow the TWSE table: 0.05 below 50, 0.10 from 50 to 100, which is why bids step by 0.05 and asks by 0.10 here.
